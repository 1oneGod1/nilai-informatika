# Grade 11 AI Studio

Fourteen 80-minute coaching sessions with individual daily targets, rather than a fixed course per meeting. English defaults with an Indonesian switch. Ten BSD progression groups contain fifteen project evidence records. Students continue from their own position after Webcam Live Classifier as reported by the teacher; earlier completed work can be submitted without repeating it.

Students enter through their existing name/password grade page. The same 12-hour session convention as the other studios is used. Teacher UI requires the teacher-preview session and matching verified Firebase teacher identity. Local synthetic preview is restricted to localhost/127.0.0.1. Classroom notes and companion quiz scores are browser-local, keyed by student ID, and never written as official grades.

Three readiness-based formative activities: F1 after Image Upload Classifier (dataset revision with fixed held-out tests); F2 after Adjustable Accuracy (KNN K=1/3/5 comparison); F3 after Find the Finish Line (finish, dead end, and retry tests). Each active formative requires one or two compressed screenshots plus a written change/prediction and test results, so a BSD share link is optional. Summative work is one meaningful project modification with before/after evidence, normal and failure tests, and an individual demonstration during a teacher-announced final window. Existing guide IDs 1–14 and personal notes are preserved for old links, but guide numbers are no longer meeting deadlines.

Daily targets are saved with the browser's local date. Project evidence and readiness marks are saved under stable BSD project names in the existing per-student namespace, alongside earlier guide notes and quiz records. A portfolio export includes all project evidence and F1/F2/F3/summative notes. Readiness is a student declaration pending teacher verification; no completion mark creates an official grade.

Course completion is grading evidence: finished required steps, demonstrable result, one test, and an individual explanation. Quality is assessed through the teacher's gradebook rubric; speed is not a criterion. The approved Grade 11 Q2 policy uses separate, versioned assessment IDs: ten course grades totaling 20%, three formative grades totaling 30%, and one summative grade worth 50%. Existing records retain their original configuration and are archived when new grading is saved. Database rules allow the two bounded JPEG screenshots on F1/F2/F3 only. Teachers announce a shared final deadline and give feedback/revision opportunities.

The complete BSD course overview allocates 25 hours. The fourteen continuation sessions provide 18 hours 40 minutes. Use targeted coaching, working Sandbox checkpoints, or independent continuation as needed. Fast students extend testing or improve a feature; students who need time continue their current target. Begin individual verification as students become ready.

Reviewed 13 October 2026: 15 revision-pinned BSD mission definitions, 163 steps, and six overview/progression/concept PDFs. Source metadata is in data.js and teacher references. Raw source downloads and synthetic QA remain outside the hosting tree. The 28 questions are original companion practice, not official BSD assessment questions or keys.

Content cautions grounded in source code: Adjustable Accuracy changes KNN K and includes randomized/scaled display confidence; count correct labels for empirical testing. Music Generator maps image labels to recorded sounds. Digital Assistant uses string if/else. Chess uses evaluation/search; navigation uses commands, conditions, pathfinding, and move memory. A variable named fastest does not prove global optimality.

After changing canonical assets, run `node scripts/prepare-grade11-assets.mjs` from the repository root. For shared student/teacher entry changes, also run `node scripts/prepare-grade12-assets.mjs` to fingerprint shared scripts. For grading changes, run `node scripts/prepare-ai-assessment-assets.mjs` to fingerprint the shared calculator, AI policy, teacher rubric UI, and grading stylesheet. Keep prior published fingerprints for already-open cached pages.

AI grading policy, approved 14 October 2026: 10 course groups × 2% = 20%, three formatives × 10% = 30%, one summative = 50%. Shared implementation: js/grade11-ai-assessment.js. Explicit teacher review is required for each component, including a graded zero. Partial saves do not replace published gradebook values. Earlier rubric records and official columns are archived on the first new-version save. All-new grading synchronizes raw F1/F2/F3, completion average, raw summative, and the combined final grade; the existing Q2 Sumatif slot carries this combined final grade and is labeled accordingly. Legacy records keep their previous display and calculations.
